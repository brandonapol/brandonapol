### From music to code

I quit teaching music in 2021 after I started learning JavaScript during lockdowns. I picked up Python and React from CodeCademy in 2020, then took a software bootcamp in 2021. About halfway through, I was offered a job teaching there upon graduation, because I had already learned the basics reasonably well and I had half a master's degree in elementary education at that point.

I wound up working as a **curriculum developer** and invented their **self-paced coursework**: a hybrid of pre-made educational videos plus "office hours" and a Slack server where students could get individualized attention. This way they weren't locked to a learning schedule or physical location, but they could get help if they needed it. I am still very proud of that product.

### Radiance Technologies

In 2023 I moved to Ohio and began working at Radiance Technologies as a "normal" software engineer.

- **Fullstack engineer** – Angular and NodeJS, then Jenkins and CI. Migrated a project from Vue 2 to Vue 3 and taught the other developers about the Composition API and functional-style programming.
- **Tech lead** – Led a project that was essentially a Tanstack/React table as a Jira plugin for database management. Made our CI and devops bulletproof, and oversaw the removal of ~50,000 lines of code, simplifying the application from about seven microservices to two.
- **Site Reliability Engineering** – Moved to authorization software and co-founded the SRE practice, a role I'm really passionate about and continue to grow in. Along with two friends, I mentor a team of about twelve engineers.
- **Agentic programming** – Spent much of the last two years helping pilot and coach agentic programming across the company.
