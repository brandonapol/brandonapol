### From music to code

I am a self-taught programmer who quit teaching music in 2021 after I started learning JavaScript during lockdowns. About halfway through a software bootcamp I was offered a job teaching there, and wound up working as a curriculum developer: I invented their self-paced coursework, which was like a hybrid of pre-made educational videos plus "office hours" and a Slack server where students could get individualized attention. I am still very proud of that product. In 2023 I moved to Ohio and began working at Radiance Technologies as a "normal" software engineer. I started as a fullstack engineer working Angular and NodeJS, then became the tech lead of a project where I oversaw the removal of around 50,000 lines of code and simplified the application from about seven microservices to two. After that I co-founded the Site Reliability Engineering practice there, which is a role I'm really passionate about, and along with two friends, I mentor a team of about twelve engineers now. I also spent a lot of time over the last two years helping pilot and coach agentic programming for the company.
